# Mochi Prompt Pack

## Base Character Prompt

Create a product-grade desktop companion mascot sprite concept inspired by a soft floppy-eared pixel character.

Character:
- tiny front-facing floppy-eared mascot
- white fur with pastel baby-blue outline
- soft pink inner ears
- small blue eye accents
- tiny blush on both cheeks
- rounded head and toy-like body
- no clothing unless extremely subtle

Style:
- high-quality pixel pet asset
- retro handheld sticker-toy charm
- cozy and soft
- pastel palette
- clean chunky silhouette
- designed for tiny desktop-pet readability

Mood:
- gentle
- warm
- harmless
- slightly shy
- companion-like

Constraints:
- no scenery
- no UI
- no text
- no floor shadow
- no glow effects
- no noisy accessories
- must feel like a real game mascot asset, not an illustration crop

## Short Art Brief

Mochi is now a floppy-eared pastel mascot companion with a white body, blue outlines, tiny blush, and a calm toy-like presence. The goal is cozy handheld-game charm with very clean readability.

## Animation-Oriented State Prompts

### Idle

Mochi floats gently in place, tiny blink, soft ear sway, calm companion energy.

### Sleepy

Mochi droops lower, eyes closed or nearly closed, sleepy and very soft, long ears resting heavier.

### Happy

Mochi brightens and bounces lightly, cheerful tiny smile, ears lift slightly but stay floppy and cute.

### Thinking

Mochi tilts subtly, one paw raised or body leaning, curious and thoughtful but still quiet.

### Coding

Mochi should feel attentive and supportive, more focused than excited, still visually calm.

### Gaming

Mochi should feel alert and reactive, slightly more energetic eyes, but remain low-noise.

### Watching Video

Mochi should look curious and entertained, with side glances or small reaction changes.

### Chatting

Mochi should feel more socially open and warm, with a brighter mouth shape and inviting face.

### Excited

Mochi becomes visibly more animated, with stronger bounce and perkier expression while staying soft and rounded.
