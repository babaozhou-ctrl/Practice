# Mochi Prompt Pack

## Base Character Prompt

Create a product-grade desktop companion mascot sprite concept based on a soft floppy-eared pastel mascot, with the same emotional read as the provided reference board: broad head, tiny body, long drooping ears, tiny face, calm toy-like front pose, and retro handheld-game charm.

Character:
- tiny front-facing floppy-eared mascot
- white fur with pastel baby-blue outline
- soft pink inner ears
- small blue eye accents
- tiny blush on both cheeks
- broad soft crown and toy-like body
- short blue top accent band or very subtle forehead stripes
- stubby feet, tucked tiny paws, very compact body mass
- no clothing unless extremely subtle
- face features must stay small and slightly low-set on the head

Style:
- high-quality pixel pet asset
- retro virtual-pet / handheld mascot charm
- cozy and soft
- pastel palette
- clean chunky silhouette
- designed for tiny desktop-pet readability
- strong sprite-sheet readability, not poster illustration

Mood:
- gentle
- warm
- harmless
- slightly shy
- companion-like

Constraints:
- no scenery
- no UI
- no text
- no floor shadow
- no glow effects
- no noisy accessories
- no catgirl anatomy
- no humanoid torso
- no maid costume or anime fashion detailing
- no tall bunny posture
- must feel like a real game mascot asset, not an illustration crop

## Short Art Brief

Mochi is a floppy-eared pastel mascot companion with a white body, baby-blue outlines, tiny blush, short forehead stripes, and a calm toy-like presence. The goal is cozy handheld-game charm with very clean readability and a stronger resemblance to a premium retro virtual companion than a generic bunny sticker.

- `reference-user-hero-crop-v3.png`

Use the newer v3 board as the main likeness lock. The v2 crops remain useful for traceability, but any disagreement should resolve toward the v3 board.

## Animation-Oriented State Prompts

### Idle

Mochi floats gently in place, tiny blink, soft ear sway, calm companion energy.

### Sleepy

Mochi droops lower, eyes closed or nearly closed, sleepy and very soft, long ears resting heavier.

### Happy

Mochi brightens and bounces lightly, cheerful tiny smile, ears lift slightly but stay floppy and cute.

### Thinking

Mochi tilts subtly, one paw raised or body leaning, curious and thoughtful but still quiet.

### Coding

Mochi should feel attentive and supportive, more focused than excited, still visually calm.

### Gaming

Mochi should feel alert and reactive, slightly more energetic eyes, but remain low-noise.

### Watching Video

Mochi should look curious and entertained, with side glances or small reaction changes.

### Chatting

Mochi should feel more socially open and warm, with a brighter mouth shape and inviting face.

### Excited

Mochi becomes visibly more animated, with stronger bounce and perkier expression while staying soft and rounded.
